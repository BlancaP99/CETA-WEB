Unmanned Aircraft Systems

Since 2007, CETA has supplied Unmanned Aerial Systems (UAS) to the Spanish Armed Forces and Law Enforcement Agencies, providing state-of-the-art systems and products featuring the latest technological advances.

CETA offers one of the most comprehensive UAS portfolios on the market, ranging from small FPV platforms to long-range tactical systems with extended endurance.

CETA’s aerial uncrewed systems, backed with millions of flight hours experience, operate in the most complex environments in air, land and sea.
